About

I'm a Filipino content creator, pastor, and digital storyteller passionate about bridging creativity, faith, and social impact. I started my career in 2016 as a full-time video editor, where I learned the art of storytelling through visuals, pacing, and emotion. Over the years, I've worked on a wide range of projects, from short films and documentaries to viral online content.

In 2018, I expanded my role into social media strategy, helping individuals, churches, and brands grow their influence through meaningful and message-driven content. That journey led me to build Khan Santos TV, my digital platform where I share faith-based, thought-provoking, and visually engaging videos that speak to both Christian and mainstream audiences.

My passion has always been to translate deep biblical truths into relatable, modern stories — mixing humor, theology, and culture in a way that resonates with today's generation. Through my work, I've been able to reach millions across Facebook, YouTube, and TikTok — not just to entertain, but to inspire transformation through creative storytelling.
